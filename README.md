## The joke

> I bought the dip at $60k, then $50k, then $40k — turns out the dip was just the stairs.

## How it runs

`main.py` is standard-library-only Python. It writes the one-liner to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

## Why this line

The setup is every trader's real life: "buy the dip" repeated at $60k, $50k,
$40k — three entries, each one confident, each one wrong in exactly the way
real dip-buying goes wrong. The reversal lands in the last clause: the dip
was never a floor to catch, it was a staircase going down. "The dip was just
the stairs" reframes the whole trade as a walk downstairs — no bounce, no
recovery, just gravity — which is why the punchline stings: the joke is
funny because the setup is true.
